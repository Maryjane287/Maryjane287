Patterns everywhere

A hundred chart shows how our numbers work: go right to add one, go down to add ten. Coloring the counting patterns makes the times tables visible.

Questions parents and teachers ask

What are the puzzle pieces?

Small pieces cut from a hundred chart with only the middle number shown. Children work out the numbers above, below and on each side.
