# PrintPals: our blue ocean

The worksheet market is crowded (Twinkl, Education.com, Teachers Pay Teachers, K5, colouring sites).
They all compete on the same thing: more worksheets. Most hide the good ones behind subscriptions,
ask for an email, show adverts, and leave parents to work out what to print.

We compete on the problems parents actually have. Each problem below has a feature that answers it.

| The parent's problem | What most sites do | What PrintPals does (live) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I don't know what to print today." | 10,000 sheets and a search box | **Weekly learning pack**: a whole balanced week in one click, at the child's age, with their name on every page |
| "I'm not a teacher. How do I help?" | Nothing | **Grown-up guide** in every pack: what each page teaches and what to say |
| "It's too hard / too easy." | Start again and search | **Easier and Harder buttons** on every levelled sheet |
| "I have two or three children of different ages." | One child at a time | **Siblings mode**: same theme, each child at their own level, side by side |
| "Ink is expensive / my printer is black and white / I print at a shop." | Full colour everything | **Ink saver** on every sheet: white backgrounds and grey pictures |
| "I type my child's name again on every site." | Accounts and logins | **Remember my child**: the name is kept on the device only, no account |
| "Grandma lives in another country." | Nothing | **Family far away pack**: postcards, video call bingo, interview questions, news page, countdown |
| "I need 20 quiet minutes right now." | Nothing | **Quick packs**: restaurant, rainy day, sick day, calm before bed, outdoors |
| "Is it safe? Who sees what I type?" | Sign ups and trackers | **Private by design**: made in the browser, fonts self-hosted, no trackers |

## Next ideas (in order of impact)
1. **Learning passport**: a printable passport a child stamps after each pack, kept going week after week (brings families back every week).
2. **Heritage language packs**: English plus a home language (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Swahili, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish...) for families abroad. Needs native speaker checking before launch.
3. **Class packs for teachers**: paste the class list, get a named pack per child at their level.
4. **Calm and dyslexia friendly mode**: bigger spacing, simpler pages, a dyslexia friendly font.
5. **Brainlings bridge**: packs end with a "tell Bibi what you learned" page and a link to the app.

## How it can earn money without losing trust
- Keep the website free and advert free: that is the promise that makes parents share it.
- Sell **printed activity books** made by the same engine (Amazon KDP, Etsy digital downloads): themed, beautifully laid out, and personalised books are a natural gift.
- Point families to **Brainlings** (the paid app) from the packs, especially the family far away pack.
- An optional "support us" link for families who want to give back.
