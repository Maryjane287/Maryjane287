Ask any grandparent who lives far away what they want and the answer is nearly always the same: more of the grandchildren. More news, more photos, more of the funny things they say. These gifts bring a little of that home.

A family newspaper

Turn the family's news into a proper old style newspaper, with headlines like "Leo loses first tooth, tooth fairy pays a whole £2". Add the kids' funniest quotes, a family weather report and your favourite photos. It is the kind of gift that ends up on the fridge, then in a special drawer forever.

A drawing a month

Post a new drawing from the grandchildren every month. Date each one so they build a gallery over the year.

Recorded bedtime stories

Ask a grandparent to read a story on a video call and record it. It is a gift for them and for the kids.

A recipe swap

Ask for a favourite family recipe, cook it together over video, and send back a photo of the result. Even if it went a bit wrong.

An all about me magazine from the kids

Let each child answer a few questions about themselves: favourite food, what they want to be, what they are an expert in. It captures exactly who they are this year, and grandparents treasure it.
