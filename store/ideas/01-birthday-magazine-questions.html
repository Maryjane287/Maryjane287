A birthday magazine is only as good as the stories inside it. The trick is asking questions that bring out the little details only you would know. Here are our favourites, grouped so you can pick and mix.

The cover story

  1. What would their celebrity nickname be?
  2. What are they secretly brilliant at?
  3. What are they obsessed with right now?
  4. Which three words sum them up?
  5. What would the headline of their year be?

The iconic moments

  1. What is the funniest thing they have ever done by accident?
  2. When did they make you laugh so hard you cried?
  3. What is a moment you will never forget?
  4. What is their most famous saying?
  5. What is the bravest thing they have ever done?

By the numbers

  1. How many cups of coffee (or tea) a day?
  2. How many times have they said "five more minutes"?
  3. How many countries have they visited?
  4. How many plants have they rescued, or accidentally not rescued?
  5. How many hours have they spent looking for their keys?

Friends tell all

  1. What is the best advice they ever gave you?
  2. What do they always order?
  3. What song do they sing loudest?
  4. What is their go to dance move?
  5. What would they grab first in a fire drill?

The heartfelt pages

  1. What do you love most about them?
  2. What has changed about them this year?
  3. What are you proud of them for?
  4. What do you hope next year brings them?
  5. What would you say if you only had one sentence?

The just for fun page

  1. What would their superhero name be?
  2. Which film character are they most like?
  3. What would their perfect day look like?
  4. What would be in their dream sandwich?
  5. What is their birthday horoscope? (It is always extremely positive.)
