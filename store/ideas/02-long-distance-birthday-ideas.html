Missing someone's birthday because of distance is hard. The good news is that the most loved gifts are rarely about being in the same room. They are about showing someone you remember the little things.

1. Send snail mail on purpose

A real envelope in a real letterbox still feels like magic. Send a letter, a pressed flower or a printed photo so it arrives on the morning of their birthday.

2. Make them the cover story

A personalised magazine filled with your shared jokes and favourite photos is something they can hold, reread and show off. Order a printed copy and it is made near them, so it arrives quickly without customs surprises.

3. Plan a video breakfast

Set up a call, each make the same breakfast, and sing happy birthday badly together. Bonus points for matching party hats.

4. Open when letters

Write a little stack of letters for them to open through the day: "Open when you wake up", "Open at lunch", "Open when you blow out the candles".

5. A playlist of your year

One song for every month you have known each other, or every memory you share. Add a note for each one.

6. Rally the group

Ask friends and family for one memory each, then gather them all in one place. A family newspaper or magazine is perfect for this, because every story gets its own headline.
