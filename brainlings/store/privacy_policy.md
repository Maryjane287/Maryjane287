# Brainlings Privacy Policy

**Grace and Loannes Ltd** (company number SC899696, United Kingdom) makes Brainlings, a learning app for children aged 4 to 7.
Last updated: 25 September 2026.

We built Brainlings for our own children and yours, so we keep this simple: **your family's information stays on your phone.**

## What Brainlings keeps, and where

When a grown-up sets up the app, Brainlings keeps on the phone only:

- the child's first name or nickname, and their age
- the names of family members the grown-up adds (for example "Mummy" or "Grandma")
- an optional recording of the grown-up saying the child's name, so Bibi can say it in their voice
- optional voice or text messages a grown-up records for the child in the grown-up area
- game progress: stars, stickers, levels and the play time limit

All of this is stored **only on the device**. We do not have servers that receive it, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location or contacts.

## The microphone

Brainlings uses the microphone in three places, and only while the child is using them:

- **Talk to Bibi:** Bibi copies what the child says in a funny voice. The sound is played back straight away and is never saved.
- **Say It!:** Bibi listens to the child's answer. To understand the words, the app uses the phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). That service may process the audio to turn it into text, under its own privacy terms. Brainlings does not record, save or send the audio anywhere else.
- **Grown-up area:** a grown-up can record the child's name or a message. These recordings stay on the phone.

## No ads, no tracking, no chat

- Brainlings has **no advertising** and no advertising IDs.
- Brainlings has **no analytics or tracking** tools.
- Children cannot chat with anyone, and no one can contact them through the app.
- There are no links out of the app for children. The grown-up area is behind a grown-ups gate.

## Deleting everything

In the grown-up area, choose **"Start again from the beginning"** to erase the child's name, recordings, messages and progress from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

## Children's privacy

We follow the Google Play Families Policy, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code), UK GDPR and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because personal information never leaves the device, we do not collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If a future version adds features that send information off the phone (for example, family members sending messages from their own phones), we will update this policy before that version is released and explain it clearly in the app.
