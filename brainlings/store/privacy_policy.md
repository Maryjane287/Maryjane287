# Brainlings Privacy Policy

**Grace and Loannes Ltd** (company number SC899696, United Kingdom) makes Brainlings, a learning app for children aged 4 to 7.
Last updated: 26 September 2026.

We built Brainlings for our own children and yours, so we keep this simple: **your child's information stays on your phone.** The only thing that ever travels online is a voice letter from a family member you invited, on its way to your child.

## What Brainlings keeps, and where

When a grown-up sets up the app, Brainlings keeps on the phone only:

- the child's first name or nickname, and their age
- the names of family members the grown-up adds (for example "Mummy" or "Grandma")
- an optional recording of the grown-up saying the child's name, so Bibi can say it in their voice
- optional voice or text messages a grown-up records for the child in the grown-up area
- game progress: stars, stickers, levels and the play time limit

All of this is stored **only on the device**. We do not create accounts, and we do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location or contacts. Your child's name, age and recordings are never sent to us.

## Letters from far away (family links)

A grown-up can choose to send a family member a private link (for example by WhatsApp). When that family member opens the link and records a voice letter:

- the recording (and any short written note) is stored on Google Firebase servers in London, UK, **only until your child's phone collects it**. It is then deleted from the servers straight away;
- your child's phone sends back a small note so the sender can see that the letter was played and whether a hug was sent back. This note contains only a number of plays and a yes or no for the hug;
- to keep this private without passwords, the phone and the family web page use anonymous sign-in: a random code, not linked to any name, email or phone number;
- only people holding a link you sent can write, only your child's phone can read the letters, and you can stop any link at any time in the grown-up area.

Your child's name is never stored on our servers. It is only inside the link itself, which you choose to share.

## The microphone

Brainlings uses the microphone in three places, and only while the child is using them:

- **Talk to Bibi:** Bibi copies what the child says in a funny voice. The sound is played back straight away and is never saved.
- **Say It!:** Bibi listens to the child's answer. To understand the words, the app uses the phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). That service may process the audio to turn it into text, under its own privacy terms. Brainlings does not record, save or send the audio anywhere else.
- **Grown-up area:** a grown-up can record the child's name or a message. These recordings stay on the phone.

## Payments

If you subscribe after the free week, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store card details.

## No ads, no tracking, no chat

- Brainlings has **no advertising** and no advertising IDs.
- Brainlings has **no analytics or tracking** tools.
- Children cannot chat with anyone. Only family members you invite can send them letters, and children can only reply with a hug.
- There are no links out of the app for children. The grown-up area is behind a grown-ups gate.

## Deleting everything

In the grown-up area, choose **"Start again from the beginning"** to erase the child's name, recordings, messages and progress from the phone. This also stops every family link and removes your family from our servers. Uninstalling the app removes everything from the phone. To ask us to delete anything else, email graceandloannesofficial@gmail.com.

## Children's privacy

We follow the Google Play Families Policy, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code), UK GDPR and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Brainlings does not collect personal information from children: your child's details and recordings never leave the device.

## Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this policy before that version is released and explain it clearly in the app.
